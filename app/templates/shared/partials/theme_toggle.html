{# Chiaro / scuro / contrasto elevato. Needs no server state: the choice lives in localStorage and is applied by the inline script in base.html before first paint. One button cycling three themes rather than three controls: it is a decision made once and then left alone. The icon names the theme you are in — sole, luna, cerchio mezzo pieno — and the title says what pressing it does; which one is showing is decided in app.css by `data-theme`, because with JavaScript off nothing here changes and the CSS already knows the state. I tre disegni sono quelli di note-condivise, tratto e non glifo: il sole del carattere (☀) e' un asterisco magro che a 17px non si legge come un sole, e cambiava forma da un carattere all'altro.
